**How to read these notes**
- **FACT** = "Cited Findings". Each item carries a source link.
- **INTERPRETATION / SPECULATION** = "Inferences". Each item is tagged [INTERP] or [SPEC].
- **Heat labels**: EARLY = not yet peaked, with the main date still ahead. DEVELOPING = in the news now, with meme formats not yet settled. ALREADY HOT-SATURATED = at or past its peak, or the meme format is already worn out. These labels are my judgement ([INTERP]), because I had no social-volume data (see the attention caveat below).

CAUTION means the joke leans on a brand or person, so it must be abstracted. NO means the joke is inseparable from a real person, brand or IP, or the topic is harmful.
