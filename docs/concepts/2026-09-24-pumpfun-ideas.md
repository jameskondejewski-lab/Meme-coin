# pump.fun meme-coin ideas (2026-09-24)

- **How these were made:** a workflow ran 3 creative angles × 5 ideas.
- **Checks:** every name and ticker was checked live against Jupiter, DexScreener and CoinGecko, then independently re-checked by a skeptic agent for collisions and IP problems.
- **Selection:** a final judge picked 10.
- **Caveat:** the market numbers come from the live brief of 2026-09-23. The dev-buy figures in these cards assume the original 0.25 SOL; the chosen launch uses 0.5 SOL (about 1.7% of supply).

## 2. Idea cards

**These apply to every card:**
- **Mint:** Token-2022, 1B supply.
- **Dev buy:** one dev wallet buys 0.25 SOL inside the creation transaction, about 8.8M tokens (~0.88%). Fees change the exact amount, so post the real number from the transaction. No other buys from any related wallet.
- **Fees:** creator-fee mode, disclosed.
- **Dev lock:** lock the dev tokens on Jupiter Lock or Streamflow. First test-lock a small Token-2022 balance to confirm the locker supports these mints.
- **Links and metadata:** metadata is fixed at creation. Create the X and Telegram accounts first so their links are in the metadata. The lock link can't be in the description, so it goes in a pinned post.
- **Contract address:** post it only after creation, and never announce an exact launch time.
- **Graduation:** never promise it. The LP lock only applies if the coin graduates to PumpSwap.

**1. Won't Budge ($BUDGE)**
- **Concept:** a basset hound sleeps on the dev's whole bag. That bag is the disclosed creation buy, and it is locked with a public time lock. The daily ritual is a post saying the dog has not moved.
- **Visuals:** an original tricolor basset lying flat over a sack stamped "DEV BAG 0.88%", with a "Zzz". It must not look like a Shiba or Doge. Avoid a sitting, head-tilted pose (Hush Puppies) and a white face with black ears (Droopy).
- **Meme format:** "Put the dog on it" edits and "Day N: the dog has not moved" posts. Any "wen dev sell?" gets a picture of the dog. Two panels: "BUDGE!" / "no."
- **Audience:** buyers following the dog rotation, diamond-hands culture, and holders who want a calm coin.
- **Why now:** dogs are +25% over 7 days (an analyst label). After $LAPTOP fell 98% in an hour and 7 of 12 memes that peaked in September fell 57–74%, a dev dump is the trigger buyers fear most. Communities reward devs who don't sell.
- **Competition and utility:** the dog niche is the most crowded, but basset tokens barely exist (one at about $3.3K). There is no utility. The lock builds trust but guarantees nothing.
- **Launch notes:** put the wallet and "0.25 SOL at creation" in the description. Lock within minutes, for example for 180 days, then post "Day 0" with the lock transaction. Publish the plan for the unlock date on day one. If the apostrophe breaks, use "Wont Budge".
- **Key risks:** the lock covers only ~0.88%, so other holders can still dump. The unlock date will attract FUD. The dog sector is crowded and the top 10 memes hold 66%. Only about 0.26% of coins graduated in June.

**2. Strategic Junk Drawer ($DRAWER)**
- **Concept:** a parody "strategic reserve" of kitchen clutter. Holders publish "Reserve Audits" of their own junk drawers.
- **Visuals:** a half-open oak drawer with a mystery key hanging out like a tongue and googly eyes on the handle screws. Beige treasury-memo type with "AUDITED" stamps. No flags, eagles or seals.
- **Meme format:** "RESERVE AUDIT #0412" (a drawer photo plus a stamped inventory), "reserve asset of the day", and a "Mystery Key Registry".
- **Audience:** traders who noticed the reserve-parody cluster, plus mainstream humor accounts, since everyone owns a junk drawer.
- **Why now:** the closest tie to the evidence in this set. The reserve and commodity parody cluster rose 124–307% over 30 days, but on very thin liquidity, so it is an early signal and not a trend.
- **Competition and utility:** it competes with GOLD, Strategic Oil, USELESS and FARTCOIN, and searches for "Strategic" are crowded (SMR, SAOS). No utility. An optional audit-image maker needs no token.
- **Launch notes:** prepare 15–20 templates before launch. Post the contract address after the creation transaction confirms, and pin the wallet, dev-buy and lock transactions. The audit thread offers no token prizes. The description says it is not affiliated with any government.
- **Key risks:** the cluster may fade before launch. It could drift into politics. Many dead coins use the "drawer" symbol, so always use the full name and contract address. Drawer photos can show mail and addresses, so remind people to crop.

**3. Tumbleweevil ($TWEEVIL)**
- **Concept:** a polite lint weevil behind your dryer drum who takes exactly one sock per load.
- **Visuals:** a fuzzy lint-gray body, curved snout and antennae, and a red-and-cream striped sock, framed by a dryer-door porthole. The eyes are solid glossy black with one highlight so he never looks like a soot sprite.
- **Meme format:** #TumbleweevilWasHere photos of real orphan socks, with a printable paper weevil. Also ransom notes, MISSING SOCK posters, and "Tumbleweevil took it" as an excuse for anything missing.
- **Audience:** mainstream and parent humor, wholesome crypto Twitter, and merch collectors.
- **Why now:** weak to moderate. Brand/IP memes rose 40% over 7 days and PENGU is 16% of the sector, so character brands get paid. That money went to established IP, though.
- **Competition and utility:** no token uses the name or ticker. The WEEVIL ticker is full of dead micros. No utility. Any merch is sold at cost.
- **Launch notes:** at launch, open an orphan-sock roll call with the printable PDF and 5–10 ransom templates. Pin the three transactions. Never use the words "odd sox" (a real sock brand).
- **Key risks:** mainstream engagement doesn't turn into holders. The name is long. People who search WEEVIL land on dead copycats.

**4. Nailed Rug ($NAILED)**
- **Concept:** a smug rug nailed to the floor with far too many nails, each nail a public fact. Tagline: "Can't be pulled. Can definitely be walked on."
- **Visuals:** a generic orange-and-teal geometric rug with woven eyes and a grinning fringe, lots of nails and a claw hammer. Avoid the Lebowski rug motif and don't let the fringe act like Aladdin's carpet tassels.
- **Meme format:** "Try to pull it" (tow trucks, horses, grandma), a running "Nail Log", and "rug pull / *nail gun*".
- **Audience:** pump.fun veterans burned by 2026 rugs and self-aware degens.
- **Why now:** 2026 trust shocks ($LAPTOP -98%, the $20M BONK DAO attack). RugCheck shows 100% LP locked on a graduated pump.fun pool, but that only applies after graduation.
- **Competition and utility:** rug-joke tokens are tiny (all under about $14K market cap) and nobody owns the image. No utility.
- **Launch notes:** the description must say "the floor can still drop." Nail Log posts #1–4 are the wallet, the creation transaction, the dev buy and the lock transaction. If $NAILRUG still checks clean on launch day, use it instead of sharing $NAILED with a dead token.
- **Key risks:** the highest reputation risk in the set. The joke can be read as a safety guarantee, and a dump by any whale becomes "the unruggable coin rugged." The LP lock only applies after graduation. The anti-rug genre is stale.

**5. Molehill ($MOLEHILL)**
- **Concept:** a star-nosed mole who treats every tiny setback as the apocalypse and digs a mountain to scream from.
- **Visuals:** a charcoal body (never brown), squinting eyes and shovel claws. The pink star nose is drawn as separate rays, even at icon size. He sits on the mound with his full body showing, not poking out of it (that looks like Diglett or Monty Mole). No big white eyes or red nose (Krtek).
- **Meme format:** a MOLEHILL vs MOUNTAIN two-panel (tiny problem on the left, absurd Everest on the right), "Molehill Reacts", and a drama Richter scale.
- **Audience:** traders who meme their own overreactions and fans of weird animal facts.
- **Why now:** mood only. Sentiment swung from Extreme Fear to Extreme Greed (78 on Sep 22), which makes this an overreaction market. Moles aren't part of the animal rotation.
- **Competition and utility:** the name is unused on the main sources, and MOLE-ticker micros are at most $38.6K market cap. No utility.
- **Launch notes:** ship the template and a Telegram sticker pack at creation. Pin a "the dev is not allowed to overreact" pledge next to the lock transaction. Never use "whack-a-mole" (a Mattel trademark).
- **Key risks:** the art can drift toward Diglett or Krtek, so lock a style guide. Search results for the common word are noisy. A dead exact-ticker MOLEHILL exists on the Robinhood chain.

**6. Insomnowl ($INSOMNOWL)**
- **Concept:** an owl who said "gn" three hours ago and is still scrolling. He is also a reminder to go to bed.
- **Visuals:** a navy egg-shaped body, huge yellow eyes, purple bags and a bedhead tuft, lit from below by his phone. The logo is just the glowing eyes. Never green, and no Duolingo streak jokes.
- **Meme format:** "INSOMNOWL O'CLOCK" clock screenshots with an eyes overlay, the "gn / still online" two-panel, "one more chart" vs sunrise, and timezone roll calls.
- **Audience:** night traders across Asian, EU and US hours, gamers and students.
- **Why now:** cultural, not money flow. Solana leads DEX volume ($80B over 30 days) with about 263K new tokens a day, and the USELESS rally came from Korean listings. Nothing shows money flowing into owl or night themes.
- **Competition and utility:** no hits anywhere. NOWL is taken by dead micros, which is why the ticker is long. No utility and no health claims.
- **Launch notes:** a late-night launch fits the joke, but don't pre-announce it. Pin the clock overlay, the sticker set and the three transactions. Run a Sunday "go to bed" thread.
- **Key risks:** it could read as glorifying sleep loss. The 9-letter ticker is hard to type. Owls are a common mascot.

**7. Tattletale ($TATTLE)**
- **Concept:** a hall-monitor parrot who snitches on the dev. A labeled bot posts real dev-wallet moves, and the parrot adds embarrassing trivia.
- **Visuals:** a chunky green-and-yellow parrot with an orange sash and a clipboard, mid-squawk, inside a speech bubble. Keep the curved beak so it never reads as the Duolingo owl or the old Twitter bird.
- **Meme format:** "TATTLE: [dev / me] just [thing]." Most posts are people confessing their own trades.
- **Audience:** on-chain readers and Telegram-first communities.
- **Why now:** communities reward bundle screening and transparent devs, X sued a bot farm on Sep 17, and bot-sized trades are common. Birds have no sector momentum.
- **Competition and utility:** no TATTLE anywhere, and parrot tokens are small. Light utility: the bot posts only the disclosed dev wallet's transactions.
- **Launch notes:** test the bot before creation. TATTLE #1–3 are the creation, dev-buy and lock transactions. Keep the bot on Telegram, or label it as automated on X.
- **Key risks:** if the bot misses a transaction, the core joke breaks. It can't prove no other wallets exist. Snitching on other holders could turn into harassment, so the rule is: tattle only on the dev and yourself.

**8. Blurbo ($BLURBO)**

- **Meme format:** "BLURBO SIGHTING #___" on any blurry photo, blurriness ratings, "too sharp, fake" call-outs, and a blur filter.
- **Audience:** absurdist X and TikTok and pet-photo posters. It needs no crypto context.

- **Competition and utility:** USELESS and FARTCOIN own absurdist attention. No utility.
- **Launch notes:** have 20+ ready-made sightings. The description disclaims any link to the Solana Foundation or Anza. Never shorten the ticker to $BLUR (Blur is CEX-listed, CoinGecko #436).
- **Key risks:** 3 dead exact-ticker "Blurbo" tokens (one has 43 holders) can confuse buyers. Anyone can copy the format without the name. The name sits close to the "Blorbo" and "Glorbo" memes.

**9. Department of Mild Concern ($MILD)**

- **Visuals:** a faded red rubber-stamp ring with a raised-eyebrow face, on beige and manila with a courier font. No seals, eagles or flags.

**10. One Slot Avocado ($UNRIPE)**
- **Concept:** an avocado that is ripe for exactly one slot.
- **Visuals:** a hard dark-green avocado with a determined squint and a stopwatch. One haloed "ripe" frame, then a slumped brown one. Nothing like Instadapp's Avocado logo.
- **Meme format:** four panels, "NOT YET / NOT YET / NOT YET / GONE", for any tiny window (parking spots, replies, tickets). Also "Ripeness Reports".
- **Audience:** mainstream food and adulting humor. Solana traders get the slot joke.
- **Why now:** it sits next to the commodity-parody cluster but isn't part of it. The slot-time hook comes from the idea's notes, not the verified brief.

- **Launch notes:** seed 10 examples that have nothing to do with crypto, and pin the template and the three transactions. Ban "not yet = still early" copy.
- **Key risks:** the format is easy to twist into price-timing hype. The ticker doesn't match the name, which hurts recall.

## 3. Top pick and why: Won't Budge ($BUDGE)

- **What we found:** Won't Budge is the only idea where the trust step, the joke and the largest live animal rotation all line up, and its name and ticker are fully clear.
- **Why it matters:**
  - In a pump-and-fade market, a dev dump is what buyers fear most. Here the required lock is the mascot, so following the integrity rules produces the daily content.
  - It claims less than Nailed Rug: "the dog guarantees nothing except that he is asleep."
- **Evidence:**
  - **Market:** dogs +25% over 7 days; 7-day median +15.7% with 81 of 99 up; Fear & Greed 78, then 71.
  - **Why a dev lock matters:** 7 of 12 memes that peaked in September are down 57–74%, and $LAPTOP fell 98% in an hour.
  - **Collision and IP recheck:** no BUDGE symbol on Jupiter, DexScreener or CoinGecko, and 0 results for "Won't Budge" or "Wont Budge". IP risk is low if the art stays clear of Hush Puppies and Droopy.
- **Uncertainty:**
  - The themes are analyst labels. The 30-day median is only +0.3%, so the dog rotation could be over by launch.
  - Dogs are the most crowded niche, and the top 10 memes hold 66% of the sector.
  - The lock covers only about 0.88%, so other holders can still dump.
  - Only about 0.26% of coins graduated in June, so the most likely outcome is no graduation.
  - I haven't confirmed that the lockers support pump.fun's Token-2022 mints.
- **Recommended next step:**
  1. Re-run the Jupiter, DexScreener, CoinGecko and GeckoTerminal checks, plus the pump.fun search, within an hour of launch.
  2. Commission original basset art that follows the guardrails.
  3. Test-lock a small Token-2022 balance on the chosen locker.
  4. Set the lock length and the unlock plan, and draft the "Day 0" post.
  5. Create the X and Telegram accounts, then launch.

  If dog momentum fades before launch, use Strategic Junk Drawer as the backup.

## 4. Dropped ideas

- **Fretzel ($FRETZEL):** IP risk. It is nearly identical to FRETZELS®, a pretzel brand (USPTO 78437134), and the idea plans pretzel-shaped merch. The fix, "Fretknot", has only had crypto checks; the web and trademark checks are still pending, so it isn't clearly safe.
- **Gullet ($GULLET):** conflicted. $GULL, another seabird coin, graduated on pump.fun a day ago ($434K market cap, $59K liquidity) and reads as its parent. The fix, "Pelicant", is only partly checked and the design overlaps Pokémon's Pelipper.
- **Nothing Up My Sleeve ($NOSLEEVE):** 6.25. Clever rather than instantly funny, a long name, and no sector tailwind.
- **Glass Frog ($GLASSY):** 6.25. The frog tailwind is real but tiny ($31M cap). The realistic art is hard to meme, "glass PnL" posts invite wallet exposure, and the weekly fee posts are a promise that could backfire.
- **Blockhash Not Found ($STALE):** 6.0. A niche Solana in-joke, at least six dead STALE tickers, and it could read as mocking Solana's reliability.
